HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Reduce the board to exactly one blob.

How to move

Click a blob to select it. Valid landing cells light up green. Click a green cell to jump — the blob you jump over is removed.

Jump rules

Jumps go in 8 directions (4 cardinal + 4 diagonal). The landing cell must be an empty saucer; the cell in the middle must contain a blob.

Controls

  • reset — reload the current level
  • solve — auto-solve from here (click again during playback to stop)
  • help — this dialog
  • generate — replace the level with a random solvable one
  • Level strip & arrows — jump to a specific level
