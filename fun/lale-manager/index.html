CONTROLS & SCREEN ACTIONS

Click players and buttons, or focus the screen and use arrows (or Q/S/I/P) and Enter. Space pauses a live match; Escape pauses or goes back. Tab moves through controls and out of the screen. In tactics, select a player then a zone. Defence, Midfield and Attack show the opposite markers; Overview offers formations and bench choices. At half-time, select a named substitute and click the player to replace.
