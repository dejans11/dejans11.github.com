About this website

Analytics privacy

This website uses Umami Cloud to understand visits to the homepage, Projects, Fun, and browser games. The statistics include page visits, referring sites, approximate location, and browser and device information.

The analytics tracker does not use cookies. This site does not send names, email addresses, account identifiers, game moves, or scores to Umami. URL query strings and fragments are excluded from page URLs sent by the tracker.

Analytics respects your browser’s Do Not Track setting. Local previews and this privacy page do not load the tracker. If you block analytics, the website and games still work.

Girl in Fire saves only its best score in your browser, with an in-memory fallback when saving is unavailable. Scores and gameplay actions are not sent to analytics. Sound starts off each time you load the game. Its separate offline package has no analytics tracker.

Learn more about Umami’s collected data and Umami Cloud’s privacy policy.
